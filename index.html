05 — Journal

From the house

12 September 2026 · Atelier

The glove factory on Rue du Faubourg

On the third floor of a former glove factory in the 9th arrondissement, coats are still cut on the square. The Rivoli pattern has sixteen pieces. Most of them you will never see.

3 September 2026 · Palette

Three notes for autumn

This season the house worked from three notes: ivory wool, oxblood calf, and a black that reads slightly brown in daylight. Nothing fluorescent. Nothing shy.

18 August 2026 · The Estate

A year in the Hudson Valley

The Hudson property is not a backdrop. It is where the knitwear is worn for a year before it is offered — a test against weather, saddles, and Sunday lunch.
